# Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive digital marketing strategy for **First Dream Academy**, a football training academy dedicated to developing young players and nurturing athletic talent.

The project aims to increase student enrollment, strengthen brand awareness, and build a strong online presence through effective digital marketing strategies. The proposed plan combines market research, audience analysis, Facebook advertising, content marketing, and visual branding to position First Dream Academy as one of the leading football academies in Egypt.

---

# Project Objectives

- Increase student enrollment by **15%** within one month.
- Increase Facebook reach and engagement by **20%**.
- Strengthen the academy's brand identity and credibility.
- Expand online visibility and attract new students.
- Generate qualified leads through Facebook advertising campaigns.

---

# Project Scope

The project includes:

- Market and competitor analysis using **SWOT** and **STP** frameworks.
- Target audience research and customer segmentation.
- Facebook Ads strategy (Messages & Lead Generation).
- Content marketing plan (Posts & Reels).
- Visual branding enhancement.
- Campaign performance measurement using KPIs.

---

# Final Deliverable

A complete digital marketing strategy including:

- Market Analysis
- Marketing Strategy
- Facebook Advertising Plan
- Content Calendar
- Branding Strategy
- Performance KPIs
- Recommendations for Future Growth
